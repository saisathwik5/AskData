Business Metrics Playbook

Official definitions from docs/metrics.md

Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)

MRR is the normalized recurring revenue normalized from active subscriptions. It is NOT the sum of historical cash payments!

CASE plan
  WHEN 'starter' THEN 29
  WHEN 'pro' THEN 99
  WHEN 'enterprise' THEN 499
END
WHERE status = 'active' AND u.is_test_account = FALSE
-- Ground Truth: $124,581.00 USD

Net Revenue

Gross cash collections minus refunded amounts, segmented strictly by currency:

SUM(amount - COALESCE(refunded_amount, 0))
WHERE status = 'succeeded' AND currency = 'USD'
